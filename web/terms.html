01Products — one enterprise, separate data

Quantive (government / business debt and portfolio platform), Qubo (AI tax assistant for businesses), and Quantive Personal (individual tax intelligence) are operated by the same enterprise with shared billing and authentication, but separate databases. Personal data never mixes with sovereign or institutional data.

02Qubo & data — privacy contract

Qubo's job is your taxes: categorization, deduction detection, estimates, and reports. Separately, Qubo operates an optional aggregate-trends program:

  • Opt-in only: nothing is contributed unless you explicitly opt in (in-app toggle). Opt out anytime — contribution stops immediately.
  • Aggregates only, with minimum bucket sizes: live brackets unlock only once enough contributors exist that no individual is identifiable (k-anonymity enforced server-side).
  • Shared, when you opt in: age group, age bracket, and aggregated signals such as "people are investing or spending more in this type of thing."
  • Never shared: names, contact details, conversations or message content, individual transactions, or any directly identifiable record.
  • Recipients see patterns, not people — time-bucketed, de-identified aggregates only. Attempts to re-identify individuals terminate access.

03Quantive Personal plans

  • Starter — $2,000/yr: tax write-off detection only, with limits (up to 3 opportunities, 10 documents, 20 intelligence questions/month). No Gov-grade Quantive market access; summary report only. Deliberately lean — useful for narrow needs, uncomfortable as a full system.
  • Personal — $5,000/yr: full tax intelligence — continuous profile, opportunity identification, document organization, personalized analysis, annual report.
  • Sovereign — $10,000/yr: everything in Personal plus Gov-grade Quantive market access for individuals (aggregated Qubo trends, sovereign-mode insights, CPA-ready export).

Plans are annual access to the system — not per-message pricing. Limits are enforced server-side. Tax rules are versioned by jurisdiction and tax year; unsupported countries receive generic guidance only, clearly labeled.

04Tax & investment disclaimers

  • Quantive Personal surfaces potentially relevant opportunities — never guaranteed deductions, savings, or eligibility.
  • Content is general information, not professional tax, legal, or investment advice. Verify against current rules or a qualified professional.
  • Every material conclusion should trace to a versioned rule (jurisdiction × tax year) or be labeled needs-verification.

05Acceptable use, security, deletion

  • One account per individual for Personal; do not share credentials or scrape aggregates to re-identify individuals.
  • You may inspect and correct profile facts and request deletion of Personal data (billing records retained as legally required).
  • We may suspend accounts for abuse, unlawful use, or attempts to de-anonymize Qubo aggregates.

06Billing

Shared Stripe-backed billing engine. Annual plans auto-renew unless canceled. Starter limits reset per documented windows; overages require upgrade, not overage fees.
