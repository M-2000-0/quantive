Compliance — non-negotiable

Holding money means following rules.

If you hold customer money, move customer money, issue accounts, or process payments, there are regulatory obligations in most jurisdictions. A fintech cannot opt out. Here's how we handle them:

🏦 Licensed banking partner

Funds held at a regulated partner bank (BaaS model). The partner handles chartering, safeguarding, and regulatory responsibility. Quantive provides the software, AI, and experience layer. Partner name disclosed at account opening and in all disclosures.

🪪 KYC / KYB + AML

Identity verification for individuals and businesses, sanctions screening, transaction monitoring, suspicious-activity reporting. Some legitimate users will be declined or asked for documents — that's the system working.

🧾 Reporting + audit trails

Tax forms (e.g. 1099 where applicable), transaction records, immutable audit logs, exportable books. Your accountant and our regulators both get clean paper.

🔒 Safeguarding + disclosures

Segregated client funds, FDIC pass-through insurance via partner (up to applicable limits), clear fee schedule ($0 where $0), living terms and privacy contract. No "no reporting" — full reporting.

FAQ

Asked honestly.

Really 0%? What's the catch?

No Quantive transaction fee. We earn from net interest margin on deposits held at our partner bank, plus optional Pro subscriptions, lending, and treasury spreads. Any third-party network costs are disclosed — we never mark them up silently.

Is this a real bank? Is my money safe / reported?

Quantive is not a bank. Banking services are provided by a licensed partner bank, member FDIC, with pass-through insurance subject to limits. All required KYC, AML, and tax reporting applies. If anyone promises "no regulation, no reporting" while holding your money — run.

How is this different from Mercury or Brex?

Same compliant BaaS foundation, but with a built-in AI CFO (bookkeeping, tax, forecasting), multi-bank view, and a direct loop into property investing (Open Doorway) and revenue growth (ARM). The account is free; the intelligence compounds.

What does Pro $49–$499/mo add?

Free = bank + auto-books + tax tags. Pro adds forecasting, AI CFO Q&A, tax optimization, advanced analytics and planning. Scale ($499) adds lending access, yield pockets, Doorway investor tooling, ARM automation, and SLA support.

Can property investors use this?

Yes — that's the point of Open Doorway. Rental payouts land with $0 fees, get tax-tagged instantly, and feed your cash forecast and lending profile. Treasury pockets hold reserves between deals.
