THE PROBLEM

THE SOLUTION

Real-time transaction monitoring — Ingest and score across Ethereum, Solana, Polygon, BNB Chain, Arbitrum, and Avalanche.

Risk scoring engine — 9 configurable dimensions (high value, mixer interaction, sanctioned addresses, new wallet interaction, rapid successive txs, unusual gas, zero-value dusting, exchange wallets, velocity checks). Every flag includes a human-readable reason code.

Alerting with context — Automatic alerts for HIGH and CRITICAL scores with transaction details, wallet profiles, and triggered rules. Route to Slack, Teams, Discord, PagerDuty, Jira, or email via webhooks.

Case management — Built-in investigation workflow with assignment, comments, escalation, and resolution tracking. Full audit trail on every action.

Compliance reporting — Generate transaction logs, risk overviews, case summaries, and audit trail exports in CSV or PDF with one click.

Role-based access — Admin, analyst, and viewer roles with granular permissions per user.

Immutable audit logs — Every action logged with unique trace IDs and timestamps.

Automation — 55+ n8n workflow templates for alert routing, report scheduling, onboarding sequences, and compliance digests.
