The problem

Manual compliance does not scale onchain.

Legacy screening tools generate excessive false positives, scatter data across silos, and require days of preparation for audits.

  • False positive overload

    Rules-only engines flag 90%+ false positives, consuming analyst hours on non-risks.

  • Fragmented data

    Wallets, transactions, alerts, and cases live across separate systems with no unified view.

  • Slow audit preparation

    Compiling audit trails and compliance reports takes days instead of minutes.

  • Volume bottlenecks

    As transaction volume grows, legacy infrastructure cannot keep pace.

How it works

From transaction to decision in three steps.

Quantive ingests blockchain data, applies risk rules, and surfaces actionable cases for analyst review — no black boxes.

download
Step 1

Ingest and normalize

Connect via blockchain APIs, webhooks, or CSV import. Data is normalized across chains into a single queryable format with wallet resolution.

speed
Step 2

Score and alert

Rule-based risk scoring evaluates every transaction across nine dimensions. High-risk events trigger alerts with specific reason codes for full transparency.

description

Review and report

Analysts investigate cases, document findings, and close with resolution notes. Generate audit-ready reports in CSV or PDF with one click.

Capabilities

Everything a compliance team needs.

Built for teams that require speed, accuracy, and complete audit trails across every action.

monitoring

Real-time monitoring

Ingest and score transactions across Ethereum, Solana, Polygon, and more. Sub-200ms assessment per transaction at any volume.

Rule-based risk scoring

Configurable engine with nine risk dimensions. Every flag includes a specific reason code. AI-assisted scoring available but never final without human review.

Case management

Built-in workflow for assigning, commenting, escalating, and closing cases. Full audit trail on every action with timestamps and actor IDs.

assessment

Audit-ready reports

Export transaction logs, risk overviews, case summaries, and full audit trails as CSV or PDF. Ready for regulators in one click.

security

Role-based access

Granular permissions per user. Admins, analysts, and viewers each see only what they need. SSO and SAML available for enterprise.

api

REST API for programmatic access. Webhooks for alerting Slack, Teams, PagerDuty, or any custom endpoint with automatic retry.

layers

Multi-tenant architecture

Fully isolated data per organization. Suitable for MSPs, multi-jurisdiction firms, and holding companies with strict data boundaries.
